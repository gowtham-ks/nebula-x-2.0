## 3. AUDIT METHODOLOGY & COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

1. **Mandatory Source Restriction**: Every claim has been cross-referenced against the NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA) and official NASA mission documentation.
2. **Status Neutrality**: Fictional status descriptions (e.g. active weather stations on retired hardware) have been eliminated.
3. **Traceability Guarantee**: Every record in `src/machinesData.js` and `data/nssdc_archive.json` embeds a discrete `sources` array specifying `id`, `title`, `publisher`, `url`, `resourceType`, `supports`, and `verificationStatus`.
4. **UI Transparency**: UI labels claiming official accreditation or certification were replaced with educational recognition terminology.
